Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

ZenLab is a personal publishing tool operated by the owner of the ZenLab accounts for their own use.

Data we access

When the account owner authorizes ZenLab with YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, ZenLab receives OAuth access tokens and basic account information (account name and ID) and the list of videos published by that account, solely to upload the owner's videos and confirm that they were published.

How data is used and stored

Tokens are stored encrypted-at-rest on a private server controlled by the account owner and are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties. No data about other users or viewers is collected.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google, Meta or TikTok account settings; stored tokens are then deleted. Requests: zenlabcore@gmail.com.

ZenLab's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
